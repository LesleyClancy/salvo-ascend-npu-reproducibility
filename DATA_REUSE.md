# Reuse of derived records

The numerical records in `data/curated/` include measurements derived from HumanEval and JFLEG workloads. The package does not include those datasets' source sentences or token sequences. The legal status and any applicable restrictions for the derived records have not been determined; no additional license grant for them is asserted. Consult `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` for upstream sources, attribution, and licence information.

The case `free_1` appears in two records. The original 55-case aggregation used e5c after sorted source records with the same identifier were overwritten; the separate policy analysis used e5a and reported e5c. Both observations are retained. The selection and its effect on the reported correlation are described in `data/curated/README.md`.
